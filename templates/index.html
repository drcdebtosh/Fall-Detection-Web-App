AI PIPELINE

How It Works: Step-by-Step

See exactly how our multi-stage detection engine processes every frame to identify falls with high precision.

01

Frame Extraction

Accepts uploaded videos, webcam feeds, or RTSP camera streams. Frames are read sequentially at native resolution and framerate, feeding the detection engine in real-time without buffering delays.

Real-timeZero-lag
02

YOLO Pose Estimation

Each frame runs through the advanced YOLO-Pose model, extracting a highly accurate 17-keypoint COCO human skeleton. It tracks multiple individuals simultaneously, even in crowded scenes.

17 KeypointsMulti-person
03

CA-EMA Smoothing

Confidence-Aware Exponential Moving Average (CA-EMA) dynamically filters the skeleton data. It reduces noisy jitter when detection confidence is low, but preserves sudden, sharp movements indicative of a fall.

Noise ReductionAdaptive
04

Multi-Signal Extraction

Our engine calculates physical metrics on the fly: descent velocity, vertical acceleration, torso angle to the ground, aspect ratio changes, and body height collapse. These physics-based signals are robust against different camera angles.

VelocityTorso Angle
05

5-State Machine Tracking

The state machine transitions from NORMAL to DESCENDING, then to IMPACT, and finally LYING. It also detects near-falls and tracks balance recoveries automatically, avoiding false alarms.

State TrackingFalse Alarm Rejection
06

Confirmation & Alert

Once a fall is confirmed, it classifies the severity (SOFT, MODERATE, SEVERE). It then monitors post-fall immobility; if the person remains on the ground for an extended time, it escalates to an emergency alert state.

Severity ScoringImmobility Escalation
